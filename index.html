How to Play

Stack it clean

Tap (or press Space) to drop each floor onto the one below. Line it up for a Perfect and build your combo. Overhang gets sliced off — miss entirely and the tower falls.

On the start screen you can switch between Endless and the shared Daily board, and between Normal and Hardcore — which adds a shot clock, spike hazards, random quakes and blackouts.
